## Don't

<!-- Convert approved exclusions into explicit governance. Keep only rules relevant to this direction. -->

- Do not introduce colors, radii, typography, shadows, or motion outside this system.
- Do not use gradients, gradient text, glassmorphism, decorative blobs, excessive pills, or ornamental animation unless this document explicitly defines their product role.
- Do not place every section inside a card; use containers only for meaningful grouping or interaction.
- Do not introduce another font or component library without an approved system-level reason.
